PROMPT 1 — Initial creation request

$blender-create-model

Create a stylized boy getting ready for school, wearing a backpack and
adjusting one shoulder strap. Give him a friendly, alert expression,
deliberate character proportions, and readable hands and shoes.

simple graphic shapes, controlled bands of shading, and dark drawn details.

Palette: muted blue and warm yellow, with restrained warm wood and skin tones.
Camera: use the default clinical inspection view when no angle is specified:
centered, full-body orthographic three-quarter view with comfortable margins.

Use blender-setup, blender-materials, blender-light-camera, blender-render,
and blender-review-optimize as appropriate alongside blender-create-model.
Work through the verified official Blender Lab MCP in the open Blender app.
Preserve unrelated Blender content and create an isolated example scene.

Deliver:
- result.blend: editable scene with final camera, lighting, and materials.
- result.png: clean 1280x720 final camera Render Result, without any Blender UI.
- editor.png: genuine Blender editor screenshot of the completed scene.
- review.md: actual skills, settings, timings, checks, revisions, and limitations.

Use 640x360 previews and target under 30 seconds per still, as a goal rather
than a hard limit. Prefer EEVEE. Inspect the actual renders and reopen the
saved scene to check portability before presenting it for human approval.
This is a static character illustration; no rig or animation is requested.
No external image reference is supplied for this pilot; use the textual style.

Success criteria: clear complete silhouette; one hand visibly adjusting the
backpack strap; friendly readable face; recognizable bedroom context; warm
light without losing facial detail; no clipped head or feet; editable scene.
