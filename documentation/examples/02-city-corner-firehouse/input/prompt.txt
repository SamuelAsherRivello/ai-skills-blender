PROMPT 1 - Initial creation request

$blender-create-environment

Create a realistic architectural visualization of a firehouse on a city
corner. Make it a detailed two-story red-brick station with two large
fire-engine bays, a pedestrian entrance, stone trim, metal apparatus doors,
reflective upper windows, a restrained station sign, and a hose-drying tower.
Include sidewalks, curbs, an intersection with crosswalks, streetlights,
a hydrant, and neighboring city buildings to establish the urban setting.

Use realistic proportions and physically based masonry, stone, concrete,
painted metal, asphalt, and glass. Add subtle surface texture and weathering.
Use natural daytime lighting, balanced exposure, and believable shadows.
Camera: clear architectural three-quarter perspective showing the front
and side of the station and its relationship to the street corner. Keep
the complete building visible and avoid excessive wide-angle distortion.

Use blender-setup, blender-procedural-geometry, blender-materials,
blender-light-camera, blender-render, and blender-review-optimize alongside
blender-create-environment. Keep repeated facade windows editable with
documented controls and verify those controls within sensible limits.
Preserve existing Blender content and use an isolated scene.

Deliver all generated files inside output/:
- result.blend: editable portable scene, camera, materials, lights, controls.
- result.png: clean final camera Render Result at 1280x720, no UI or HUD.
- editor.png: genuine Blender editor screenshot of the completed scene.
- review.md: settings, actual skills, measured timings, verification,
  corrective actions, assumptions, and remaining quality limitations.

Use 640x360 previews. Target less than 30 seconds per still as a goal,
not a hard limit. Benchmark EEVEE or GPU Cycles and use denoising when useful.
The result should read as realistic architecture, not a cartoon miniature.
No external image reference is required: this is an original fictional
firehouse, not a survey or reconstruction of a particular real building.
Verify the saved file by reopening it and inspect the actual output images.
