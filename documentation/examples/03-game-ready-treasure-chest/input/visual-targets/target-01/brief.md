# Treasure chest visual direction

Generated concept input, not a Blender render. One generation, inspected visually.

Priorities: broad domed lid over a rectangular walnut box; clearly separated boards; dark iron bands with bright brass rivets; central brass clasp; small turned brass feet; warm left key and cool right fill. Complete object, contact shadow and front/right-side view are readable. Main body width roughly 1.7 times body height; domed lid adds substantial height.

Target carries four longitudinal lid bands (two interior plus end edging) and extra side handle. These are optional inspiration, not requirements. Surface realism is stronger than the requested stylization: simplify grain/weathering and maintain readable game silhouette. Do not chase fine texture detail at the cost of the triangle budget.

Prioritize silhouette and material separation, then bands/clasp/rivets. Hidden rear hinges and underside are inferred, not measured. A single image cannot verify mesh count, UV quality, baking, export or reimport: check those in Blender independently. Use the genuine render for comparison; no actual Blender evaluation was performed by this worker.
