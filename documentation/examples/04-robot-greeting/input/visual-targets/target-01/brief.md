# Robot greeting visual direction

Generated concept input, not Blender output. One generation only; inspected complete image.

Observed: rounded teal shell, yellow ear discs, cuffs and broad boots; black inset face with two cyan happy arc eyes; yellow ball antenna. Oversized head, compact torso, narrow waist and exposed dark joint bearings. Raised hand has four visible segmented digits; the other hand hangs beside the torso. Feet contact a neutral gray floor with soft shadows. Full subject fits; top antenna margin is modest rather than generous. Mostly frontal three-quarter clinical pose.

Priorities for the single Blender pass:
1. Keep the large rounded head and friendly screen eyes readable, with the full wave silhouette inside the camera.
2. Separate shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip and knee parts at meaningful editable bone pivots; rigid toy components need not deform like flesh.
3. Use teal shell, yellow accents and dark joints; broad highlights and bevels convey molded toy surfaces.
4. Ground both boots. Keep framing wider than this concept so motion cannot clip the raised hand or antenna.
5. Animate a clear elbow/wrist greeting while maintaining planted feet. The still cannot demonstrate rig quality, temporal motion, playable preview or GLB export.

Assumptions: unseen rear shell and joint mechanics are simple symmetric continuations; exact dimensions are design choices. Chest ring, panel seams and four-digit hands are incidental generated details, not additional requirements. Favor readable major forms over small seams under the time budget. No 3D comparison has been performed by this worker.
