# Low-poly island visual target

Status: ready as direction, with extra generated details excluded from requirements. One generation; no Blender comparison performed by this worker.

Observed: a complete rocky island in turquoise water, warm pale beach, roughly three stepped grassy terraces, planar gray cliffs, conical pine trees, a readable S-shaped sandy path and a white lighthouse with a small red roof. The elevated three-quarter orthographic-like view reveals the terrain hierarchy. Dark pine greens and the red focal accent balance saturated cyan water. Sunlight from upper left creates soft readable shadow planes.

Priorities for the one authorized construction pass:
1. Preserve the complete island silhouette and clear water margin, including the lighthouse tip.
2. Establish stepped faceted cliff masses and warm beach before fine details.
3. Make the winding path visibly continuous toward the lighthouse; vary pine height and grouping without blocking it.
4. Use flat shaded planes with restrained material variation, a red lighthouse roof, warm sunlight and soft contact shadows.

Generated additions are optional inspiration only: waterfall, rock arch, dock, boat, fences, companion keeper house and outlying stacks were not requested. Do not expand scope to reproduce them. The lighthouse is taller than the word 'small' may imply; prioritize a small focal lighthouse consistent with the user prompt. Its railing, stairs and window details can be simplified for the render-time budget.

Inferred rather than measured: terrain is a stack of editable irregular rings; hidden back cliffs and lighthouse interiors can be simple. Water need not use simulation. A low-poly water plane with color variation is sufficient. The concept does not prove terrain topology, navigable paths or physical scale.
