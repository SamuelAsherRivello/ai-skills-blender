## Prioritized 3D cues
1. Preserve the square grassy clearing and perimeter tree layout before increasing detail. Island width roughly four to five tree heights; exposed side depth about one tree height. These are visual estimates, not measured dimensions.
2. Build an irregular turf/soil lip over large fractured rock pillars, a tapered broken underside, dangling roots and separated debris. Distinct warm brown soil and cool gray stone make the island's torn structure legible.
3. Use clustered broadleaf crowns with individual leaf-scale facets and spreading trunks/roots. Pines need layered, drooping radial bough silhouettes rather than simple cones. Keep all trees and materials editable.
4. Give the grass a patchy yellow-green-to-deep-green palette with sparse clumps. Keep the center open; a few stones, small flowers and bushes in the target are optional decoration rather than required new content.
5. Make the shallow stream visibly continuous and downhill, with rounded bank stones, turquoise-blue water and narrow white waterfall streaks. Its unseen upstream source is an inferred simplification.
6. Use warm upper-left daylight, soft shadows and pale neutral world color. Aim for realistic roughness and microtexture on deliberately stylized large forms.
