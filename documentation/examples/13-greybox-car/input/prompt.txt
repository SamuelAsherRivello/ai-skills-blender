$blender-create-greybox

Create a minimal car greybox that communicates vehicle silhouette, wheel placement,
and ground contact. Use editable primitive-like geometry only: a ground plane, a
rectangular chassis, a smaller cabin mass, and simple wheels. Use neutral greys
only to separate functional masses. Do not model doors, windows, lights, seats,
handles, trim, bevels, texture maps, or UVs.

Deliver an editable Blender source, a modest clean render, and a self-contained
GLB for the Model Viewer. State what was intentionally omitted.
