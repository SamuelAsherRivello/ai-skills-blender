# Car greybox review

This greybox answers whether the vehicle's wheel placement, ground contact, and two main masses read as a car. It uses a chassis block, cabin block, four low-sided wheel cylinders, and a ground plane only.

It intentionally omits all vehicle surface detail, glazing, interiors, trim, textures, UVs, and modifiers.

## Verification

The saved 960×540 render was inspected visually: all four wheels meet the ground plane and the chassis/cabin silhouette reads as a car. The adjacent GLB was opened in a local browser viewer, where the body masses, wheel geometry, and ground plane were present and orbit controls were available. Public HTTP access remains unverified because this example has not been published.
