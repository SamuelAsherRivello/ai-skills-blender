$blender-create-greybox

Create a minimal city greybox that communicates blocks, varied building height,
and a primary road crossing. Use an editable ground plane, simple road planes,
and varied-height rectangular building masses only. Use neutral greys only to
separate ground, road, and buildings. Do not add facades, windows, sidewalks,
vehicles, street furniture, trees, texture maps, bevels, or UVs.

Deliver an editable Blender source, a modest clean render, and a self-contained
GLB for the Model Viewer. State what was intentionally omitted.
