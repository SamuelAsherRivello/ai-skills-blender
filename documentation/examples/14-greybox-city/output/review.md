# City greybox review

This greybox answers whether a primary road crossing, four city blocks, and a varied-height building hierarchy read immediately. It uses one ground plane, two road planes, and six rectangular building masses only.

It intentionally omits facades, roads markings, sidewalks, vehicles, street furniture, trees, texture maps, UVs, and modifiers.

## Verification

The saved 960×540 render was inspected visually: the crossing roads remain visible between the grouped building blocks and the varied heights are in frame. The adjacent GLB was opened in a local browser viewer, where the ground, road crossing, and all building volumes were present and orbit controls were available. Public HTTP access remains unverified because this example has not been published.
