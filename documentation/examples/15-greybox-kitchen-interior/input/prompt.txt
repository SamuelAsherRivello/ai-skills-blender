$blender-create-greybox

Create a minimal interior greybox of a house kitchen. Use an open room shell
with a floor and two walls, then add simple counter and refrigerator masses to
show the kitchen work zone and circulation. Use neutral greys only to separate
room boundaries, work surfaces, and appliance mass. Do not add cabinets,
doors, windows, handles, sink detail, stools, decoration, texture maps, bevels,
or UVs.

Deliver an editable Blender source, a modest clean render, and a self-contained
GLB for the Model Viewer. State what was intentionally omitted.
