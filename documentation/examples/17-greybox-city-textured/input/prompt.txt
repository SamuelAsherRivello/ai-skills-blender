$blender-create-greybox

and a primary road crossing. Use a ground plane, road planes, and varied-height
rectangular building masses only. Apply the skill's shared Slate category materials
with a restrained procedural checker as a scale and grouping cue. Do not add facades,
windows, sidewalks, vehicles, street furniture, trees, image textures, bevels, or UVs.

Deliver an editable Blender source, a modest clean render, and a self-contained
GLB for the Model Viewer. State the category-to-material map and omissions.
