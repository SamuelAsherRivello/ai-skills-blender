$blender-create-greybox

floor and two walls, then simple counter, refrigerator, and island masses to show
the work zone and central circulation. Apply the skill's shared Slate category
materials with a restrained procedural checker as a scale and grouping cue. Do not
add cabinet fronts, doors, windows, handles, sink detail, stools, decor, image
textures, bevels, or UVs.

Deliver an editable Blender source, a modest clean render, and a self-contained
GLB for the Model Viewer. State the category-to-material map and omissions.
