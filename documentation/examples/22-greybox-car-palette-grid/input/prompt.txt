$blender-create-greybox

Create a minimal car greybox using a fixed light greybox palette. Use exactly two
categories in their introduction order: floor first and car second. The car body
and all faces of its four wheels share the second material; do not create a wheel
category. Use a shared world-aligned one-metre grid with square tiles. Do not add
glazing, doors, lights, interiors, trim, image textures, UVs, or modifiers.

Deliver an editable Blender source, render, and self-contained GLB with the
palette/category map and grid method documented.
