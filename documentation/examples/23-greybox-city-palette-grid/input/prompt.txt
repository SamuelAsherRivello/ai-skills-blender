$blender-create-greybox

Create a minimal city greybox with a primary road crossing and varied-height
building blocks. Use exactly two palette categories in introduction order: the
ground and road surfaces first, then every building second. Use a shared
world-aligned one-metre grid with square tiles. Do not add facades, windows,
road markings, sidewalks, vehicles, trees, image textures, bevels, or UVs.

Deliver an editable Blender source, render, and self-contained GLB with the
palette/category map and grid method documented.
