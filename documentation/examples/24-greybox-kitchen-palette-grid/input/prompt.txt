$blender-create-greybox

Create a minimal house-kitchen interior greybox. Use exactly three palette
categories in introduction order: floor first, both walls second, then all
kitchen furniture/appliances third. The counters, island, and refrigerator share
that third material. Use a shared world-aligned one-metre grid with square tiles.
Do not add cabinet fronts, doors, windows, handles, sink detail, stools, decor,
image textures, bevels, or UVs.

Deliver an editable Blender source, render, and self-contained GLB with the
palette/category map and grid method documented.
